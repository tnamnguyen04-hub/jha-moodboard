Type, printed on real paper

06 · FILM-STILL COLLAGE

Film stills around his head

Picture vibe taken frame by frame from the reference reel Jha loves, rebuilt on his C0029 footage with money and high-ticket pictures. Each frame mirrors a moment in the reel and loops its timing: pictures pop on one per beat, hold, then hard-cut off.

THE PICTURE VIBE
1
Emotional film stills
Pictures feel like frames from 70s–90s movies: people mid-emotion, laughing, worried, screaming. Never stock, never screenshots.
2
Intimate close-ups
Extreme crops on an eye, lips, teeth, hands. One black-and-white close-up gets handwriting and sparkles.
3
Warm red, heavy grain
Everything leans red-orange with lifted blacks and film grain. Deep red is the recurring color: the rose, the word block, lipstick.
4
Flush, overlapping, bleeding
Big: 40–50% of the frame wide. Plain rectangles, no border or shadow, ringing the face or cascading diagonally over it and the body, running off the edges.
5
Cut-out objects, no outline
A rose, a flower or a hand cut cleanly and dropped over the shot, or alone on black, spinning towards camera.
6
Text poster and red word block
A moody photo with a short white sans line on it, and a solid red square with one typewriter word.
7
Full-screen cutaways
Now and then the whole frame cuts to a film or meme clip for one beat, then back to the talking head.
8
Serif words, chapter titles
One cream serif word at chest height, a stacked serif hook, and numbered chapter titles with an object behind them.

THE AESTHETIC
Quiet luxury, not collage. Each slide is one full-bleed candid photo: cars, studios, penthouses, a desk at night. Natural light, a little underexposed, the person small in the frame or not in it at all.
The photo carries the mood; the type does the talking. No stickers, tape, halftone or paper here, which keeps carousels distinct from the reels.
Structure: hook with a “here's exactly what I'd do” promise, five “I would…” lessons, an offer slide, then one CTA keyword to comment.
LAYOUT · 1080 × 1350
Text block top-left, 86 px margins, starting 150 px from the top, same spot on every lesson slide so swiping feels steady.
Dark fade from the top (about 80% black, gone by 60% down) keeps white text readable on any photo, plus a soft text shadow.
Light variant for bright photos: white fade from the bottom, Ink text in the lower half, keyword in Signal Orange.
Hook and CTA are centred; the hook mixes small lines with one huge word.
